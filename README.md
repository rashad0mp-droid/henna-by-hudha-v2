# Henna by Hudha — website preview, version 2

A second, completely restyled take on the single-page preview for Henna by Hudha, a mehendi
artist in Kozhikode & Malappuram, Kerala. Not yet the official website. Version 1 lives in
`../website` and is untouched.

Same facts, photos and features as version 1; only the design is new.

- Look: flat colour blocks (her logo's lavender, wet-paste oxblood, fresh-stain orange,
  unbleached cotton) joined by a scalloped henna border, with one type family (Anek, by
  Ek Type) used condensed for headlines, expanded for labels and plain for text.
- The big word at the top is "mailanchi", Malayalam for henna. It starts stain-orange and
  deepens to dark, the way a stain does.
- Hero prints: tap the stack to send the top photo to the back.
- Work: her six approved photos as labelled prints (swipe on a phone, wall on a desktop).
- Try-on: unchanged from version 1. Hand tracking runs on the visitor's device with Google
  MediaPipe Hands; no photo is uploaded. Designs in `designs/` are cut-outs of her own work.
- Enquiry: a fill-in-the-blanks sentence that opens WhatsApp with the message ready.
- The mandala behind the enquiry section is drawn by code, a little different on each visit.
- Edit the brand spelling and WhatsApp number in the `SITE` config at the top of the script
  in index.html.
- Everything unconfirmed shows a yellow CONFIRM tag on the page (her story, client words).
